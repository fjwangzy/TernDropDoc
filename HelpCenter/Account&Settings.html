1. Sign up/log in TernDrop account ​

Click the “Register” button on the TernDrop main page.

Proceed to the registration page and fill in your usename, email address,full name, and password. After all the blanks are filled in, click the “Register Now” button to complete the registration process.

If you have any questions about the registration or logging in, please contact us through the online customer service or support email.

Customer Service Email : support@terndrop.com

2. Change & reset TernDrop account password ​

Your registered email can not be changed once it is registered.

If you want to recover your password, click "Forgot"on the reset password page. Just enter your email address and you will receive the verification code, then fill in new password and confirm new password.Click "confirm"to reset your password.

If you want to reset your password, you can login your terndrop account enter Membership Center & Change Password, you will be able toreset password in this page.

3. Shipping address filling ​

You can log in terndrop account and click the right top part in your account name, enter Membership Center & Shipping Address. Then follow the prompts to fill in information including receipient, contact phone number, postal code, and detailed delivery address.

4. TernDrop account profile information ​

You can log in terndrop account and click the right top part in your account name, you will be able to see the profile page, you can edit your company name, address, and contact informatio. After you complete the editing, click save button to complete the process.
