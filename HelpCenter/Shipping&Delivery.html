1. How to select the shipping line for products ​

On the product detail page, you can select a logistics company. Currently, we support shipping lines provided by YunExpress. As the official logistics service provider of TernDrop, YunExpress offers a wide range of shipping options, supporting the transportation of general cargo, products with built-in batteries, cosmetics (liquid & powder).

Please make sure to choose a logistics route that matches the nature of your product. For example, products with batteries must select a logistics route that supports battery transportation. Otherwise, your order cannot be shipped successfully.

2. International shipping information tracking ​

Click "Order Details" and click the "Fulfillment Log" in the order detail pade. You will be able to see the tracking information there.

3. International Shipping Estimating ​

On the product detail page, in the International Shipping Estimate module, you can enter the product weight and dimensions, then select the logistics company and logistics route. The international shipping cost will be calculated automatically for you.
