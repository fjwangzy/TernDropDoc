1. Sourcing ​

You can search by keywords or paste an image URL on the product search page, also can search by Image.

After entering the search list page, you can sort by sales volume and price. Click on the product to enter the product detail page and view more detailed information about the item.

Contents and Icon Definitions：

11,000 sold : means this product has been sold 11,000 units in the recent 30 days.

Repeat Pruchase Rate: the percentage of repeat customers to total customers.

Super Factory: The highest membership level on the 1688 platform, referring to large-scale manufacturers with strong production capacity and technical strength. They are usually industry leaders, enterprises above designated size, or specialized and sophisticated enterprises. They focus on displaying production equipment, manufacturing processes, and quality control, and provide large-scale customized production services.

Qualified Factory: Refers to small and medium-sized manufacturers, wholesalers, or traders with a certain scale and commercial reputation. They emphasize stable supply chain resources, product display, and customer service.

2. Order ​

On the product detail page, you can place an order directly or add the product to the shopping cart. Meanwhile, the procurement cost breakdown of the product is available on the product detail page, including Product Price, Domestic Shipping, International Shipping, and Agent Procurement Service Fee.

International Shipping Fee can only be calculated after completing the basic product information, destination country, and logistics company and route selection.

3. Payment ​

Click "Buy Now" enter the order confirmation page. After confirming all information is correct, click "Place Order", a prompt will appear indicating that the order was submitted successfully.

Enter Membership Center & Purchase Order, click "Pay Order" to make payment. Two payment methods are currently supported, PayPal & Bank Transfer.

4. Order status ​

Possible order status: "Not Started" , "In Progress" , "Completed" , "Cancelled".

If you want to know the sub-status,you can click "Fulfillment Sub-status",there will show more status about the oder, which can let you know more details.
