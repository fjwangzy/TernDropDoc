How does TernDrop work ​

Now you already know about TernDrop, let's see how it works. We will sync your Shopify/WooCommerce orders and handle all steps including sourcing, quality checks, value-added services, package processing, and global shipping until delivery to customers.

Order Sync ​

Any order customers place on your Shopify/WooCommerce store will be auto-synced to TernDrop Admin. ​

Sourcing ​

TernDrop will auto-generate purchase orders, source items from Chinese marketplaces, and follow up to ensure timely delivery to our warehouse. ​

Package Processing ​

TernDrop auto-submits packages, then we'll take items off shelves, pack them and handing them over to couriers. ​

Logistics Shipping & Tracking ​

Packages are shipped overseas, and TernDrop supports real-time tracking updates, with shipping info auto-synced to your Shopify/WooCommerce store until delivery to customers. ​
