### 1. Sourcing

You can search by keywords or paste an image URL on the product search page, also can search by Image.

After entering the search list page, you can sort by sales volume and price. Click on the product to enter the product detail page and view more detailed information about the item.

### 2. Order

On the product detail page, you can place an order directly or add the product to the shopping cart. Meanwhile, the procurement cost breakdown of the product is available on the product detail page, including Product Price, Domestic Shipping, International Shipping, and Agent Procurement Service Fee.

International Shipping Fee can only be calculated after completing the basic product information, destination country, and logistics company and route selection.

![1773391386537 6208f251 f50c 4dd6 bd23 29512d80cfff](https://media-rs.oss-cn-hongkong.aliyuncs.com/obsidian/2026/05/11/1773391386537-6208f251-f50c-4dd6-bd23-29512d80cfff.png)

### 3. Payment

Click "Buy Now" enter the order confirmation page. After confirming all information is correct, click "Place Order", a prompt will appear indicating that the order was submitted successfully.

![1773391476279 4c3073d4 831c 436e be70 fab83f68e70e](https://media-rs.oss-cn-hongkong.aliyuncs.com/obsidian/2026/05/11/1773391476279-4c3073d4-831c-436e-be70-fab83f68e70e.png)
